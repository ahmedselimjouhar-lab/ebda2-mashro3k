- A visible caution is shown when more than one occupational profile has a near-identical interest-rank score.

Limitations
- This is implementation QA, not psychometric validation. Synthetic runs do not establish reliability, validity, or occupational prediction accuracy.
- The Arabic prompts and provisional ranking rules have not been normed or validated on Egyptian or wider Arab samples.
- O*NET is a U.S. occupational reference. It does not establish Egyptian labor demand, wages, credentials, licensing, or local opportunities.
- The 23 project ideas are fixed starter templates ranked against user inputs; they are not generated from a live Egyptian market database and are not validated commercial opportunities.
- Budget, time, runway, and opportunity-fit weights are provisional product rules, not scientifically established coefficients.
- Live Vercel deployment was not performed. The site owner must replace the project files and deploy the package.
- The package is a static front-end. Answers are stored locally in the browser; there is no WhatsApp submission or external backend in this version.
